## Caveats

- Rizzo et al. curves are parametric fits (V_t, R_t, beta, xi) to lensed [CII] cubes at 20-300 pc resolution; six radii per galaxy are sampled from the smooth curve with a 10 % floor, so the chi2 values are indicative, the r profiles are what matters. Their asymmetric-drift term uses sigma0 = 50-150 km/s and is large for SPT0113-46 and SPT0441-46.
- ALPAKA rings were read off a 937 x 1020 pixel figure (2-5 rings per galaxy, 10-15 % errors), sizes from the mass-size relation: weak individual constraints, kept for completeness; x_out is large for most (Newtonian regime), so H_A and H_B differ little.
- Lelli et al. 2023 rings were read off their Figure 7 (CO lines only); structure from their HST fits.
- The law's maximum outer slope for a pure exponential disc is +5 % between 3 and 6 Rd; rising curves beyond that are not a0.
