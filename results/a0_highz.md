# a0 at high redshift: expansion-tied or constant? (2026-10-11)

Law g_obs = nu(g_bar/a0) g_bar with nu(y) = 1/(1 - exp(-sqrt(y))), a0(0) = 1.2e-10 m/s^2, fitted for r = a0(z)/a0(0) on every galaxy with a total and a baryonic acceleration at the same radius. H_A: r = H(z)/H0 (Omega_m = 0.3). H_B: r = 1. MUSE: r = (1.0 + 1.59 z)/1.2 (Ciocan et al. 2026). chi2 uses the quoted errors, the sensitivity of the law to g_bar, and an intrinsic scatter of 0.08 dex. 68 % intervals from Delta chi2 = 1. Systematics: shift of log r when all baryonic masses move by +/- 0.2 dex (sys_M) or all velocities by +/- 10 % (sys_V).

## Published constraints used as they stand

- MUSE-DARK III (Ciocan et al. 2026, A&A, arXiv:2604.22613): 79 SFGs, 0.33 < z < 1.44, log M* > 8.8; RAR fitted on model rotation curves gives a0 = 2.38 (+0.12/-0.10) e-10 at z ~ 1 and a0(z) = (1.0 + 1.59 z) e-10, from 1.99e-10 in the lowest z bin to 2.71e-10 in the highest. Baryons = stars + a constant-surface-density HI disc; no molecular gas. The authors state that a0 = 1.2e-10 at all z would need stellar masses larger by +0.2 dex (lowest bins) to +0.45 dex (highest), i.e. gas fractions of 37 to 65 % of the baryons, which is what Tacconi et al. 2018 give for log M* = 9-10 at z = 1-1.4 before any HI. Their evolution is degenerate with the gas they do not include.
- Puech et al. 2010 (A&A 510, A68): bTFR at z ~ 0.6 with V_flat from 3D modelling, zero-point shift -0.05 +/- 0.08 dex (no evolution within 0.08 dex). H_A predicts -0.14 dex at z = 0.6 (r = 1.39), H_B 0.
- Milgrom 2017 (arXiv:1703.06110) on the six Genzel et al. 2017 galaxies: g(R_1/2) = 3-11 a0, observed phantom fractions 0.07-0.21 match MOND with the local a0; a value ~4 a0 at z ~ 2 is 'all but excluded' (would give fractions ~0.5).
- Lelli et al. 2023 (A&A 672, A106): two CO discs at z = 1.47 and 2.24, flat to 8 kpc, fitted with the local a0 and the local interpolation function with mass-to-light ratios 10-30 % below the Newtonian baryons-only fit; consistency, not a measurement of a0.
- Ubler et al. 2017 (ApJ 842, 121): bTFR zero point -0.44 dex at z ~ 0.9 and -0.27 dex at z ~ 2.3 relative to Lelli et al. 2016, with v_circ,max at 2.2 Rd: the sign and the non-monotonic z dependence are the signature of v_circ,max > V_flat in compact high-acceleration discs, not of a0; the direct fit above uses the same data with sizes.
