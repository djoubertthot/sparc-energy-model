# External-field effect at 0.3-2.5 Mpc: QUMOND against the lensing circular velocities of Mistele et al. 2024 (2026-10-12)

Isolated KiDS-1000 lenses, four baryonic-mass bins, circular velocities from the spherical deprojection of the stacked excess surface density (statistical and systematic errors in quadrature). QUMOND prediction for a point mass in a uniform Newtonian external field e_N (Gauss's law on the field equation, monopole). The authors do not trust their isolation criterion beyond 1 Mpc; results are given for R <= 1 Mpc and for the full range.

## Reading

- Reference field: the 2M++ baryonic Newtonian field of the SPARC galaxies is e_N = 1.2-2.0e-4 a0 (median 1.5e-4, Milky Way 1.3e-4), of which only 3.7e-5 comes from within 10 Mpc; an isolation criterion at 3 Mpc cannot bring an isolated lens below about 1e-4.
- The lowest-mass bin (log M_b = 10.1, the most sensitive because r_t is proportional to sqrt(M_b)) is flat to 1 Mpc and gives e_N < 5e-05 a0 at 2 sigma; the 2M++ field is excluded at Delta chi2 = 14 (3.8 sigma) in that bin alone. The three heavier bins are compatible with 0 and with 1.5e-4 (their r_t lies at 0.7-1.5 Mpc, where the data stop).
- Combined over the four bins, R <= 1 Mpc: e_N < 6e-05 a0, the 2M++ field excluded at 3.9 sigma.
- Either the law holds in the lensing regime without the external-field effect of a MOND-type field theory (a dark component, or a non-local law whose field equation has no EFE), or the isolated KiDS lenses of 10^10 Msun sit in external fields three times weaker than the median 2M++ field of SPARC galaxies, which the isolation criterion does not provide. QUMOND/RMOND with the EFE is in tension with this data at the 3 sigma level.
- Caveats: point-mass baryons (fine beyond 50 kpc); the stacked, deprojected V_c measures the monopole of the QUMOND effective mass, which is what Gauss's law gives; no two-halo term (Mistele et al. estimate it small below 1 Mpc for their isolation criterion, and the result holds for R <= 1 Mpc alone); systematic errors added in quadrature and treated as independent between radii, so the bound is optimistic by perhaps a factor 1.5; the hot-gas content of the lenses raises M_b and the inner amplitude (absorbed by the free amplitude) but not the shape beyond r_t. A dedicated stack with the external field estimated per lens from the KiDS/GAMA environment would turn this into a measurement.
