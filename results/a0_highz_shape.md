# a0 at high redshift, third pass: the shape of the outer rotation curves (2026-10-12)

r = a0(z)/a0(0); H_A: r = H(z)/H0 (2.3 at z = 1.5, 6.9 at z = 4.4); H_B: r = 1. The shape test compares the measured outer slope of the rotation curve with the slope the law predicts for the galaxy's own baryonic disc, the baryonic mass being free with a 0.3 dex prior: it is insensitive to alpha_CO, M/L and the gas fraction at the 0.3 dex level.

- g_bar from M* + molecular gas (Tacconi et al. 2018, median M_mol/M* = 1.21), exponential disc; g_bar/a0 at Re: 16-50-84 % = 1.13 / 1.61 / 2.51; nu_obs median 1.93, law H_B 1.39, H_A 1.77.

### Same, no pressure correction, molecular gas in a disc twice as extended as the stars

- v/sigma0 >= 3.0 (N = 3): t law H_B median 1.007, H_A 1.031, observed median 1.04; r = 1.35 (0.58-3.72); H_B Delta chi2 +0.1, H_A +0.4
- v/sigma0 >= 1.5 (N = 11): t law H_B median 1.020, H_A 1.040, observed median 1.03; r = 1.66 (0.91-3.39); H_B Delta chi2 +0.7, H_A +0.5
- deep-MOND limit of the slope for a single exponential disc: t = [M(<6Rd)/M(<3Rd)]^(1/4) = 1.05; the law cannot produce t > 1.05 whatever a0, so slopes above it measure the pressure correction, the extrapolation of the fitted curve beyond R_Halpha,max (3-4 Rd for most KURVS discs) or an extended gas disc, not a0.

## z ~ 4.4, shape of the four Roman-Oliveira et al. 2024 curves (mass free, 0.3 dex prior)

- combined: r = 0.10 (0.10-0.35); chi2(H_B) = 15.1, chi2(H_A) = 28.5, min = 11.2: H_A at Delta chi2 = +17.4, H_B at +3.9.

## Shape test, all (KURVS v/sigma0 >= 1.5 with pressure correction + four z ~ 4.4 curves)

- KURVS: H_A - min = +85.5, H_B - min = +133.8 (N = 11)
- z ~ 4.4: H_A - min = +17.4, H_B - min = +3.9 (N = 4, 17 rings)
- total: H_A at Delta chi2 = +102.9, H_B at +137.7 above the best shape fit.

Caveats: the KURVS slopes are H-alpha rotation velocities with v/sigma0 = 1.5-4.4; the pressure correction adds 6-12 sigma0^2 to V_c^2 and is itself uncertain by tens of percent (30 % assumed here), which is why the v/sigma0 >= 3 subset and the no-correction variant are shown; beam smearing and extrapolation to 6 Rd are handled by the authors' models. The z ~ 4.4 curves are [CII] circular speeds after asymmetric-drift correction, 3-5 rings each.
