# Residual Energy as a Separate Source

## Approach
Treat f_missing as an additional energy source whose value depends on known galaxy properties.

## Tests to distinguish origin
- **Internal**: correlates with luminosity, star formation rate, dust mass
- **External**: correlates with volume, environment, or is more uniform

## Functional form
$$E_\text{missing} = E_0 \times f(\text{age}, R_d, \text{type})$$
