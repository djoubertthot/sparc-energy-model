# Logical Factor from Theory

## Residual Pattern
Residuals increase strongly with V_flat and luminosity.

## Theoretical Proposal
The missing energy could scale with the dynamical energy or the depth of the potential:
\[
f_{\rm missing} \propto V_{\rm flat}^2 \quad \text{or} \quad f_{\rm missing} \propto M / R
\]

## Justification
A deeper potential or higher orbital energy may allow more efficient temporary mass increase or longer residence of the unknown energy.

## Test
Try f_missing ∝ (V_flat / 150 km/s)^δ with δ ≈ 1–2.
