# Temporary Energy Input and Low-Acceleration Amplification

## Idea
A temporary increase in effective mass does work on the baryonic system. In regions of low acceleration the dynamical response (change in orbital energy) can be relatively larger, producing an effective amplification.

## Plausibility
Qualitatively attractive, but no quantitative derivation currently shows that this mechanism naturally yields the observed (a0 / a_bary)^β form.

## Status
Possible conceptual link, but not yet demonstrated.
