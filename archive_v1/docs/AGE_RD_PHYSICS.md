# Age + Rd and Physical Factors

## Connection
- **Age** : longer accumulation time for both known and unknown energy sources; more dust production.
- **Rd** : smaller galaxies have higher average density → longer trapping / more collective reprocessing.

## Mapping to existing factors
- f_accumulation ∝ (age / 10 Gyr)^α
- f_piegeage further modulated by 1/Rd

## Consistency
Both dependencies are already present in the physical narrative; the combination simply makes them explicit.
