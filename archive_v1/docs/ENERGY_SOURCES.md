# Amplification vs External Energy Sources

## Plan

### 1. Internal Amplification
- Estimate maximum realistic trapping time for photons/neutrinos
- Compare required amplification (~10^8) with physical limits

### 2. External Energy
- CMB energy density ≈ 4.2×10^{-14} J/m³
- Neutrino background energy density (smaller)
- Integrate over galactic volume and cosmic time
- Compare with required E0

### 3. SPARC Comparison
- Convert any viable energy density into an equivalent ρ
- Compare resulting WRMS with the calibrated model

## Status
These are order-of-magnitude estimates to be refined locally.
