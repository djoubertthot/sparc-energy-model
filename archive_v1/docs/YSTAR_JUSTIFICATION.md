# Y* Justification

## Formula
\[
Y^*_{\rm eff} = \frac{Y^*_{\rm SPS}}{f_{\rm retention}} \times f_{\rm IMF}
\]

## References
- Lelli, McGaugh & Schombert (2016), AJ 152, 157 — SPARC
- McGaugh & Schombert (2014), AJ 148, 77 — Y* at 3.6 μm
- Meidt et al. (2014), ApJ 788, 144 — stellar mass-to-light ratios
- Kroupa (2001), MNRAS 322, 231 — IMF
- Bruzual & Charlot (2003), MNRAS 344, 1000 — SPS models

## Status
Physically motivated reference value Y*_eff ≈ 1.0.
