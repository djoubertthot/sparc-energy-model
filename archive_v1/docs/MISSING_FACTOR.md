# Reaching the Missing Factor of ~550

## Possible Mechanisms
1. **Strong trapping / multiple scattering**
   - Photons or neutrinos remain in the galaxy for a time >> R/c
   - Required trapping factor ~550 relative to free-streaming

2. **Unknown energy source**
   - Dark sector, modified vacuum, or new particle
   - No observational evidence at present

3. **Phenomenological calibration**
   - The current model already sets E0 to the required value
   - No need for an explicit source

## Status
No known physical mechanism naturally supplies a factor of 550 without additional assumptions. The calibrated phenomenological model remains the practical solution.
