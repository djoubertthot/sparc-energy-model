# Why RMS Correlates with Morphological Type

## Observation
RMS is higher for early-type galaxies.

## Reasons
1. Early-types are more massive and have higher V_flat (absolute residuals larger).
2. They often have bulges, bars, and more complex mass distributions.
3. Relative residuals are more comparable once velocity is accounted for.

## Status
Consistent with the V_flat correlation and expected from kinematic complexity.
