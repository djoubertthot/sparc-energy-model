# Continuous Mass Evolution Applied to All Mass Terms

## Principle
The mass of the galaxy is treated as continuously evolving. All terms that depend on baryonic mass (a_bary, accumulation, trapping) use the time-dependent mass.

## Implementation
- Present-day dynamics use current mass
- Accumulation integrals use historical mass
- Trapping efficiency uses historical density

## Status
Conceptual framework. Full implementation requires time-resolved mass assembly histories.
