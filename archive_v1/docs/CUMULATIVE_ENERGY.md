# Cumulative Known Energy Budget

For a 10^{10} M☉ galaxy over 10 Gyr:

| Source | Energy (J) |
|--------|------------|
| Stellar radiation | ~1.2 × 10^{54} |
| CMB + neutrinos | ~2 × 10^{54} |
| Hawking radiation | ~10^{-28} |
| **Total known** | **~3 × 10^{54}** |
| Required (m_res = M_bary) | ~1.8 × 10^{57} |
| **Missing factor** | **~600** |

All known sources together still leave a factor of several hundred missing.
