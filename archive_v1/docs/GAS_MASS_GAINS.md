# Gas Quantities and Mass Gains

## Gas
Recommended factor ×2.0 on the gas contribution.

## Disk and Bulge
Y* = 1.0 already includes an elevated mass-to-light ratio for both disk and bulge.

## Combined
Total baryonic mass is increased relative to standard Y* = 0.5 + gas×1 by a factor of approximately ×1.5–2 depending on the gas fraction.

Current recommended values.
