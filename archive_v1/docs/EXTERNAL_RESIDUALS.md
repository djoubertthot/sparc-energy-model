# External Residuals

## Definition
Residuals at radii > 20 kpc, where the model systematically underestimates the observed velocity by ~50–60 km/s on average.

## Possible Causes
- Incomplete outer gas
- CGM not fully included
- Warps and non-circular motions
- Need for additional mass or modified dynamics

## Status
Acknowledged limitation of the pure baryonic model.
