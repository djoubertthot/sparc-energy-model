# Why Y* = 1.0 Is the Best

## Empirical Reason
It minimizes the global RMS and removes the systematic residual trends with mass and type.

## Physical Interpretation
It corresponds to the upper end of the allowed stellar mass uncertainty, effectively providing the baryonic mass required by the dynamics under the present energy model.

## Comparison
All tested alternatives (variable Y*, extra gas, combinations) give higher RMS.
