# Residual Energy Resembles a Cosmological Background

## Observation
The residual energy source performs best when treated as roughly constant across galaxies.

## Interpretation
This behavior is consistent with a cosmological background (uniform energy density) rather than a source tied to individual galaxy properties.

## Nuance
It does not prove the source is the CMB or CνB (those are too weak), but it suggests the unknown component is distributed similarly to a cosmological field.
