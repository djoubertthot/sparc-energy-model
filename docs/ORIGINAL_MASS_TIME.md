# Original Mass and Time Variation

## Idea
Predict the original stellar mass and its evolution over time, then apply the time-dependent mass to all terms in the formula that depend on baryonic mass.

## Potential Application
- Accumulation term uses time-averaged mass
- Present-day a_bary uses current mass
- Trapping efficiency depends on historical density

## Status
Conceptual. Requires stellar mass assembly histories for the SPARC galaxies.
