# Additional Factors and Proxies

## Galactocentric Distance
Already partially captured by the radial form of f_piegeage (depends on a_bary(r)).

## Local Density
Surface density Σ(r) or volume density could modulate trapping.

## Other Proxies
- Gas fraction
- Specific angular momentum
- Environment (group vs field)
- Stellar population age (when available)

## Recommendation
Test local density and gas fraction as additional modulators on the radial model.
