# Full Formula

## Current Structure
\[
E(r) = E_0 \times \left(\frac{a_0}{a_{\rm bary}(r)}\right)^\beta \times f_{\rm age} \times f_{\rm Rd}
\]

## With Time Integral
The accumulation part of E0 or the historical mass can be written as an integral, while the present-day amplification remains based on current a_bary.

## Note
The integral replaces only the time-dependent part, not the entire formula.
