# Realistic and Testable Improvements

## 1. Trapping Refinement (realistic)
- Use observed infrared optical depths from dust maps
- Limit trapping to 10⁵–3×10⁵ years
- Expected gain: ×2–5

## 2. Additional Sources (realistic)
- Cosmic rays: measurable but small contribution
- Extragalactic background light: partially included
- Expected gain: ×1–2

All proposals are based on observable quantities.
