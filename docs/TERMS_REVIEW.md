# Review of Formula Terms

## Current Formula
\[
\rho_{\rm eq}(r) = \rho_0 \left( \frac{a_0}{a_{\rm bary}(r)} \right)^\beta
\]

## Term Status

| Term | Current status | Physical motivation | Next test? |
|------|----------------|---------------------|------------|
| ρ₀ | Optimized (1e-24) | Amplitude of energy density | Already gridded |
| β | Optimized (1.4) | Strength of low-acceleration amplification | Already gridded |
| a₀ | Fixed (1.2e-10) | Cosmological scale | Keep fixed |
| Y★ | Fixed (0.5) | Stellar M/L | Minor variations possible |
| Age proxy | Tested, little gain | Accumulation time | Real ages still missing |
| Kinetic term | Negligible effect | E_k → mass | Needs better normalization |
| Surface density | Mild improvement | Trapping efficiency | Worth refining |

## Residual Issues
- Systematic positive bias (model too low at large R)
- Higher scatter than MOND / BTFR
- Extended and high-inclination galaxies harder to fit

## Suggested Next Steps
1. Refine surface-density dependence of ρ₀
2. Test a slightly higher β or a radius-dependent transition
3. Quality-filtered sample (Q ≤ 2, 40° < i < 75°)
4. Continue searching for real stellar ages
