# Progress: From 10^8 to ×3–5

## Summary
The original phenomenological energy density was ~10^6–10^8 times the known cosmic radiation backgrounds. With Y* = 1.0 and E0 = 0 the remaining discrepancy with other probes is reduced to a factor of ~3–5.

## Interpretation
Major reduction of the unexplained component, though not yet zero.

Documented as current progress.
