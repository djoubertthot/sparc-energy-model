# Historical Mass Gain and Effective Y*

## Typical Gain
Standard mass-loss models give a factor of ~1.3–1.5, bringing Y* from 0.5 to ~0.7.

## Exceeding 1.0
To reach or exceed Y*_eff = 1.0 purely from mass loss would require unrealistic retention fractions (< 0.5) for typical ages.

## Conclusion
Historical mass gain alone does not simulate more than ~0.7–0.8. Additional IMF or other effects are needed to reach 1.0.
