# Residual Gravitational Energy from Temporary Mass

## Idea
A temporary mass increase Δm(t) does work on the baryonic system. When the radiation leaves, part of the acquired mechanical energy remains as residual kinetic or potential energy.

## Formalization
\[
E_{\rm residual} \approx \eta \int \Delta m(t) \, \Phi(t) \, dt
\]
or, for an order-of-magnitude estimate:
\[
m_{\rm residual} = \frac{\eta \, \langle\Delta m\rangle \, \langle\Phi\rangle \, t_{\rm acc}}{c^2}
\]

where Φ is the gravitational potential and η is an efficiency of conversion into lasting orbital energy.

## Status
This is a conceptual formalization. Numerical tests on SPARC require a time-dependent or average potential model.
