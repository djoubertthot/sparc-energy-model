# Attaching Residual Regression to Physics

## Mathematical Observation
Residuals correlate with type, luminosity, and Rd.

## Physical Interpretation
- Type and luminosity → proxy for past star-formation intensity and dust production
- Rd → proxy for compactness and therefore trapping efficiency

## Proposal
Interpret the regression coefficients as an effective trapping efficiency that depends on the galaxy’s star-formation history and present-day density.

## Status
Conceptual link established. Further calibration needed.
