# The CMB Problem

## Measurement
The cosmic microwave background acoustic peaks constrain the baryon density Ω_b ≈ 0.05 and the total matter density Ω_m ≈ 0.3.

## Ratio
Ω_m / Ω_b ≈ 6.

## Implication
Even if all baryons are accounted for, the CMB requires approximately six times more total matter than baryons alone.

## Status
Independent of galactic rotation curve modeling.
