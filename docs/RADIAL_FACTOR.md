# Radial Factor Exploration

## Idea
Instead of a galaxy-wide factor, apply a local factor f(r) that depends on local density, acceleration, or optical depth.

## Possible Forms
- f(r) ∝ Σ(r)
- f(r) ∝ 1 / a_bary(r)
- f(r) = 1 - exp(-τ(r))
