# Varying Speed of Light Instead of Expansion

## Idea
A time-varying speed of light has been proposed in some alternative cosmologies.

## Current Status
Not part of standard cosmology. The CMB acoustic peaks, supernova distances, and BAO are interpreted within an expanding universe.

## Implication for the Model
Outside the scope of the galactic energy model. Would require a complete re-derivation of cosmological constraints.

## Status
Noted as an alternative idea, not currently adopted.
