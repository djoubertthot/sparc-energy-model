# Possible Explanations for High Residuals in Massive Galaxies

## Within the Energy-Trapping Theory
1. **Deeper potential** : more efficient temporary mass increase or longer residence time of energy.
2. **Higher past dust content** : early-types had more intense star formation and metal production in the past.
3. **Longer accumulation time** : older stellar populations.
4. **Collective effects** : denser environments allow more reprocessing cycles.

## Other Possibilities
- Incomplete baryonic mass models (bulge, stellar M/L)
- Non-circular motions in massive systems

## Recommendation
Test a term that scales with potential depth (V² or M/R) or with a historical dust proxy.
