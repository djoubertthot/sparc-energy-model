# Complete Formula for the Predicted Velocity

## Energy Density
\[
E(r) = E_0 \times \left(\frac{a_0}{a_{\rm bary}(r)}\right)^\beta \times f
\]

## Equivalent Mass Density
\[
\rho_{\rm eq}(r) = \frac{E(r)}{c^2}
\]

## Predicted Velocity
The equivalent mass is added to the baryonic mass and the circular velocity is computed from the total mass profile.
