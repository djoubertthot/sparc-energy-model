# Deprojection and the Energy-Mass Model

## Observational vs Physical

Deprojection is an **observational procedure**, not a physical effect of the energy accumulation model.

- Observed line-of-sight velocities are corrected by 1/sin(i) to recover the intrinsic circular velocity.
- Uncertainties in this correction grow rapidly as i → 90°.
- These uncertainties affect the input Vobs that the model is compared against.

## Within the Relativistic / Energy Model
The model itself (E = γ m c² → equivalent density) is formulated in the rest frame of the galaxy and does not depend on the observer’s inclination. 

There is no direct relativistic “deprojection” term in the current phenomenological formula. Any inclination dependence seen in the RMS is therefore attributed to:
1. Data quality / deprojection errors
2. Possible selection effects in the SPARC sample
3. Not to a new physical prediction of the energy-mass idea

## Conclusion
Deprojection remains an observational issue. It does not acquire a special meaning inside the relativistic energy accumulation framework.
